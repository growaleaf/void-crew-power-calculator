Power budget

Void Crew's power system is a loadout budget, not a live per-second drain: each installed piece of equipment has a fixed "Power Required" cost, and your ship's Power Core has a maximum capacity (raised by installing Power Generators). Install more than the core can supply and it overloads — heat builds up and circuit breakers trip over time, eventually shutting systems down. Engine thrust and Booster are a separate, unrelated stat and are not part of this budget. No ship's base core capacity is published anywhere the wiki documents, so enter the number from your own Power Core / Ship Management screen.

Sources & last verified date

All pages below fetched and re-read directly 2026-10-16.

  1. voidcrew.wiki.gg — Frigate — Engine Power (forward) 2000, Booster Power (forward) 4000; three medium (2x1) hardpoints plus two small (1x1); "ideal for crews of 1-3 players." No power-core-capacity figure on the page.
  2. voidcrew.wiki.gg — Destroyer — Engine Power (forward) 1000, Booster Power (forward) 2000; six medium (2x1) hardpoints plus one large (2x2); "ideal for crews of 3-4 players." No power-core-capacity figure on the page.
  3. voidcrew.wiki.gg — Arc Shield — Power Required 2 / 3 / 4 across Tier 1 / 2 / 3, data credited to "Dragon" via a Discord link.
  4. voidcrew.wiki.gg — Confessor Carronade (the page formerly titled "Shuriken Carronade") — Power Required 4 / 5 / 6 across Tier 1 / 2 / 3. The page states its own stats table may be outdated since patch 1.0 (2024-11-25).
  5. voidcrew.wiki.gg — Power Generator — Base Production +1 / +2 / +4 across Tier 1 / 2 / 3 (before enhancement slots), no unit given on the page.
  6. voidcrew.wiki.gg — Ship Management — describes the power-core-overload mechanic (circuit breakers, heat-per-second by overload level) but is marked a stub and gives no base capacity figure for any ship.

A second independent source for the per-equipment Power Required figures and for any ship's base Power Core capacity was searched for (Steam Community discussions under appid 1063420, general web search) and not found — every figure traced back to the same wiki contributor credit. That is why this page asks you to type in your own numbers rather than pre-filling them.
